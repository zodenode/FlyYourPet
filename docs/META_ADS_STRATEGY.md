# FlyMy.Pet — Meta Ads Strategy

**Platform:** Facebook & Instagram  
**Objective:** Drive qualified leads to Telegram bot for pet relocation and rescue sponsorship  
**CTA:** `https://t.me/FlyMyPetBot` | Rescue: `https://t.me/FlyMyPetBot?start=sponsor`

---

## 1. Campaign Objectives

| Objective | Primary | Secondary |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| **Awareness** | UAE expats considering relocation | EU adoptive families |
| **Consideration** | Expats with pets, mid-planning | Rescue sponsors |
| **Conversions** | Bot starts, form completions | Sponsor inquiries |

**Conversion event:** Link click to Telegram (primary). Track bot /start usage via UTM or bot analytics.

---

## 2. Audience Strategy

### Core Audiences

| Audience | Size | Targeting | Use Case |
|----------|------|-----------|----------|
| **UAE Expats** | Custom | Geo: UAE (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah) · Interest: Relocation, Expats · Age: 25–55 | Cold, primary |
| **Pet Owners UAE** | Lookalike 1–2% | Pet care, dog/cat, relocation interests | Warm |
| **EU Pet Adopters** | Custom | Geo: Spain, Portugal, Germany · Interest: Pet adoption, animal rescue | Rescue sponsors |
| **GCC Crisis Aware** | Interest | Animal welfare, rescue, charity | Rescue cold |

### Retargeting Audiences

| Audience | Definition |
|----------|------------|
| **Engaged (video 75%)** | Viewed ad video 75%+ |
| **Link Clickers** | Clicked CTA, did not complete bot |
| **Landing Page Visitors** | Visited flymypet.com |
| **Bot Starters** | Started bot (via UTMs if trackable) |

---

## 3. Funnel & Creative Sequencing

| Stage | Goal | Placement | Creative | Duration |
|-------|------|-----------|----------|----------|
| **Cold** | Awareness, problem hook | IG/FB Feed, Stories | Static (Concept 4) or Carousel (Concept 3) | 7–14 days |
| **Warm** | Emotional connection | Reels, Stories | Video Reunion (1) or Abandoned (2) | 7 days |
| **Convert** | CTA, urgency | Feed, Retarget | Carousel Card 4, Video end card | Ongoing |
| **Retarget** | Second chance | All | Static (Concept 4), “Still wondering?” | 14 days |

### Creative Flow

```
Cold → Static/Carousel (problem hook)
  ↓
Warm → Video (emotional proof)
  ↓
Convert → Retarget with CTA focus
```

## 4. Budget & Bidding

| Phase | Daily Budget | Objective | Optimization |
|-------|--------------|-----------|--------------|
| **Test** | €15–30/day | Traffic / Link Clicks | Lowest cost |
| **Scale** | €50–100/day | Conversions (if tracked) or Link Clicks | Cost per result |
| **Retarget** | 20–30% of total | Link Clicks | Lowest cost |

**Bidding:** Start with **Lowest cost** for Link Clicks. Move to **Cost cap** when volume allows.

## 5. Ad Set Structure

### Campaign 1: Expat Relocation

- **Objective:** Traffic (Link Clicks) or Conversions
- **Ad Sets:**
  - Cold: UAE Expats, broad
  - Warm: Pet Owners UAE (LAL 1–2%)
  - Retarget: Engaged + Link Clickers
- **Creatives:** Carousel 3, Static 4, Video 1

### Campaign 2: Rescue Sponsorship

- **Objective:** Traffic (Link Clicks)
- **Ad Sets:**
  - Cold: EU Pet Adopters, GCC Crisis Aware
  - Retarget: Engaged
- **Creatives:** Video 2 (Abandoned → Rescued), Carousel Card 1
- **CTA URL:** `?start=sponsor`

---

## 6. Creative Specs & Hook Rules

| Format | Specs | Hook Rule |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| **Video** | 9:16, 15–30s | First 1.8–3 sec = emotional beat |
| **Carousel** | 1:1 or 4:5 | Card 1 = 70% of impact |
| **Static** | 1080×1080 or 1080×1350 | Headline < 40 chars |

**Sound-off first:** 80% watch silent — burn captions into creative.

---

## 7. Tracking & Measurement

| Metric | Target | Tool |
|--------|--------|------|
| CTR | > 1.5% | Meta Ads Manager |
| CPC (Link) | < €2 (cold) | Meta Ads Manager |
| Bot starts | Track via /start | Telegram bot analytics |
| Conversion rate | Bot → Complete onboarding | Internal CRM |

**UTM parameters:**

```
?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=expat_relocation&utm_content=video_reunion
```

Use `utm_content` to differentiate creatives.

---

## 8. A/B Test Plan

| Test | Variants | Metric |
|------|----------|--------|
| Headline | “Just Telegram” vs “UAE → Europe” | CTR |
| CTA | “Get Started” vs “Open Telegram” | CTR |
| Creative | Video vs Carousel | CPA |
| Audience | UAE broad vs Expats interest | CPA |

Run each test 7 days minimum, 100+ conversions per variant.

---

## 9. Seasonal & Crisis Angles

- **Relocation season:** Q2–Q3 (summer moves) — increase budget
- **Crisis messaging:** When GCC pet abandonment spikes — lean into Concept 2, Carousel 1
- **Holiday:** Avoid Dec 20–Jan 5 (low intent)
