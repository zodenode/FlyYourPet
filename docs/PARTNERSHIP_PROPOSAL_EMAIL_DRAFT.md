**Purpose:** Propose a white-label/sub-agent or referral partnership to UAE pet relocation partners.  
**Use:** Customise the bracketed placeholders before sending. Use BCC if sending to multiple partners.

## Email Body

**To:** [Partner contact email]  
**From:** [Your name], FlyMy.Pet / Yureka Media  

---

Dear [First name / Team],

I'm reaching out on behalf of **FlyMy.Pet**, a Telegram-first pet relocation platform helping UAE expats and rescue sponsors move pets to Europe (Spain, Portugal, Romania, and beyond). We're exploring partnerships with established UAE-based pet relocation providers and would like to discuss a collaboration with [Company Name].

### About FlyMy.Pet

- **Model:** Customers onboard via our Telegram bot (@FlyMyPetBot). We handle document review, vet coordination, export permit facilitation, and end-to-end relocation management.
- **Routes:** UAE (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah) → EU destinations.
- **Volume:** Growing pipeline of qualified leads who have completed our onboarding flow.
- **Parent company:** Yureka Media.

### Partnership Opportunity

We're looking for UAE partners who can:

- Handle **export permit applications**, **MOCCAE health certificates**, and **ground handling** at origin.
- Provide **wholesale or sub-agent rates** for clients we refer.
- Support **manifest cargo** and **excess baggage** relocations for cats and dogs.

In return, we can:

- Send a steady stream of **pre-qualified, document-ready** customers.
- Manage customer communication and expectations via Telegram.
- Focus our outreach on EU-bound relocations that match your core routes.

### Proposed Next Step

We'd welcome a 15–20 minute call to:

1. Understand your capacity and preferred partnership structure (white-label, referral fee, or volume discount).
2. Share our current volume and pipeline.
3. Agree on a simple commercial framework (e.g. per-pet rate or % of completed relocation).

Would [specific date/time] or [alternative date/time] work for a brief call? If you prefer, we can also start with a short email exchange to align on terms.

Best regards,  

[Your name]  
[Your title]  
FlyMy.Pet / Yureka Media  
[Your email]
