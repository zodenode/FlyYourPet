# FlyMy.Pet — Price Packages & Margin Model

**Confidential — Internal Use Only**

## Cost Assumptions (Third-Party, UAE → Europe)

Based on negotiated/wholesale rates with UAE partners. Medium cat / small dog baseline.

| Service | Low (wholesale) | Mid | High (large dog) | Notes |
|---------|-----------------|-----|------------------|-------|
| MOCCAE export permit | €40 | €65 | €100 | Gov fee + partner facilitation |
| Vet: health cert + EU Annex IV | €120 | €200 | €320 | MOCCAE-approved vet |
| IATA crate | €100 | €180 | €380 | Small to XL |
| Airline cargo | €350 | €550 | €900+ | Route + weight dependent |
| Ground handler (UAE) | €120 | €200 | €350 | DXB/AUH pickup, docs |
| Ground handler (EU) | €120 | €200 | €350 | BIP clearance, customs |
| Doc review / admin | €30 | €65 | €100 | If outsourced |
| **Total Full Relocation** | **€880** | **€1,460** | **€2,450** | |

## Margin Sensitivity

If third-party costs run **10% higher** than assumed:
- Basic: margin drops ~€14 (minimal impact)
- Full (small): margin drops ~€110
- Full (large): margin drops ~€200

**Action:** Lock wholesale rates with 1–2 UAE partners before committing to displayed prices.
