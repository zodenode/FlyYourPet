# FlyMy.Pet — Best-Performing Concept Ads (IG & Facebook)

**Based on:** Meta creative benchmarks, pet-relocation industry research, emotional storytelling best practices  
**Target:** GCC pet crisis — UAE expats relocating, rescue sponsors, adoptive families in Europe  
**CTA:** `https://t.me/FlyMyPetBot` | Rescue: `https://t.me/FlyMyPetBot?start=sponsor`

---

## Research Summary: Why These Formats Win

| Format | IG Benchmarks | FB Benchmarks | Best Use |
|--------|---------------|---------------|----------|
| Video | 2.28% CTR, 5.46% CVR | 2.88% CTR | Emotional connection, mid-funnel |
| Carousel | 20–30% higher CTR vs static | 30–50% lower CPA | Storytelling, guilt → relief |
| Static | — | — | Cold traffic, retargeting, simplicity |

**Critical rules:** Hook in first 1.8–3 seconds. Design for sound-off (80% watch silent). 15–30s video optimal. First card = 70% of carousel impact. UGC/native-style outperforms polished by 4x on prospecting.

## Concept 1: Video — Owner + Pet Reunion

**Format:** 9:16 vertical | 15–30 seconds | IG Reels, FB Reels, Stories  
**Goal:** Hope, relief, social proof. Converts mid-funnel and warm audiences.  
**Funnel:** Warm / Retarget

### Hook (0–3 sec)
Open on the moment of reunion — pet rushing to owner, or owner kneeling to embrace. No logo. No setup. Pure emotion first.

**Visual direction:**
- Real footage preferred (UGC or customer-submitted)
- Handheld, natural lighting, slight movement
- Airport arrivals, home doorstep, or pet transport pickup
- Dog or cat — both resonate

### Script / Sequence

| Time | Visual | Audio / Caption |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 0–3s | Pet bolts toward owner. Owner drops to knees, arms open. | Caption: *“We made it.”* |
| 3–8s | Hug, tail wagging, tears/laughter. | Optional VO: *“She flew from Dubai. He’s here.”* |
| 8–12s | Wide shot: owner + pet walking away (airport/home). | Caption: *“No stress. No confusion.”* |
| 12–18s | Brand end card. | Caption: *“We made it possible.”* |
| 18–22s | CTA slide. | **“Start on Telegram.”** + `t.me/FlyMyPetBot` |

### End Card Copy (burned-in)
```
We made it possible.
Start on Telegram.
→ t.me/FlyMyPetBot
```

### Production Notes
- **Sound-off first:** All key lines as burned-in captions
- **Sound-on bonus:** Soft piano or hopeful acoustic; human voiceover = trust
- **Avoid:** Stock footage, over-polished studio look

## Concept 2: Video — Abandoned → Rescued

**Format:** 9:16 vertical | 20–30 seconds | IG Reels, FB Reels, Stories  
**Goal:** Empathy, urgency, sponsorship intent. Drives rescue sponsors and expat guilt.  
**Funnel:** Cold (rescue audience) / Warm (expat audience)

### Hook (0–3 sec)
Open on the “before” — pet alone, shelter, or rescuer holding animal. Visceral, not graphic.

**Visual direction:**
- Single pet’s journey in 4–5 beats
- Beats: Alone/abandoned → Rescue/shelter → Prep (crate, vet) → Flight → New home/family
- Real rescue partners or anonymized UGC
- Dog or cat with clear “story”

### Script / Sequence

| Time | Visual | Caption / VO |
|------|--------|--------------|
| 0–3s | Pet in shelter / alone. Eyes to camera. | *“He had nowhere to go.”* |
| 3–8s | Rescuer with pet. Crate prep. | *“A home waited in Germany. But who flies him?”* |
| 8–14s | Airport, crate, flight. | *“We did.”* |
| 14–20s | New family at door. Pet greeting them. | *“Now he’s home.”* |
| 20–26s | End card. | *“Don’t leave them behind. Sponsor a flight.”* |
| 26–30s | CTA. | **“Start on Telegram.”** + `?start=sponsor` |

### End Card Copy (burned-in)
```
Don't leave them behind.
Sponsor a flight — start on Telegram.
→ t.me/FlyMyPetBot?start=sponsor
```

### Psychological Angle
- **Empathy:** One pet, one story (not “hundreds” — more personal)
- **Urgency:** “Who flies him?” creates gap; “We did” fills it
- **Relief:** Journey ends in safety and love

### Production Notes
- Partner with UAE rescues for real footage (with permission)
- Avoid shock imagery; focus on hope in the resolution
- Music: Sparse → hopeful build

## Concept 3: Carousel — “Don’t Leave Them Behind”

**Format:** 1:1 or 4:5 | 4 cards | IG Feed, FB Feed  
**Goal:** Guilt → relief. Converts expats and hesitant planners.  
**Funnel:** Cold / Warm

### Why Carousel
- 70% never swipe past card 1 → card 1 = problem/hook
- Cards 2–3 = process (reduces anxiety)
- Card 4 = CTA (relief + action)

### Card Structure

| Card | Headline (≈40 chars) | Primary Text | Asset |
|------|----------------------|--------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Don’t leave them behind. | Moving? Hundreds of pets get left in the UAE every year. Yours doesn’t have to. | `carousel-1-dont-leave-them-behind.png` |
| 2 | Just Telegram. | Open @FlyMyPetBot. Answer a few questions. We handle permits, vets, flights. | `carousel-2-just-telegram.png` |
| 3 | We get them home. | Dubai → Madrid. Abu Dhabi → Berlin. Full coordination. You fly, or we fly them. | `carousel-3-we-get-them-home.png` |
| 4 | Start now. | One message. No stress. Your pet, in Europe. | `carousel-4-start-now.png` |

### Primary Text (Full Versions)

**Card 1:**
> Moving? Hundreds of pets get left in the UAE every year. Yours doesn’t have to.

**Card 2:**
> Open @FlyMyPetBot. Answer a few questions. We handle permits, vets, flights.

**Card 3:**
> Dubai → Madrid. Abu Dhabi → Berlin. Full coordination. You fly, or we fly them.

**Card 4:**
> One message. No stress. Your pet, in Europe. Start on Telegram.

### Visual Consistency
- Same color grade and font across cards
- Cohesive story: problem → solution → outcome
- 1080×1080 min (1:1) or 1080×1350 (4:5)

## Concept 4: Static — Single Headline + One Image

**Format:** 1:1 or 4:5 | Single image | IG Feed, FB Feed  
**Goal:** Clarity, low-friction CTA. Cold traffic and retargeting.  
**Funnel:** Cold / Retarget

### Headline Options (A/B test)

| # | Headline | Use Case |
|---|----------|----------|
| 1 | Just Telegram. We handle the rest. | Primary — simplicity |
| 2 | Pet relocation. One chat. Done. | Busy professionals |
| 3 | UAE → Europe. We fly your pet. | Geo-specific |
| 4 | Permits. Vets. Flights. All in one place. | Anxiety reduction |

### Primary Text (Ad Copy)

**Option A (Short):**
> Moving with your pet? Start a chat with @FlyMyPetBot. We coordinate documents, permits, and flights. UAE to Europe, stress-free.

**Option B (Retargeting):**
> Still wondering how to bring your pet? One message on Telegram. We’ll walk you through every step.

### Image Direction
- **Hero:** `static-just-telegram-we-handle-the-rest.png` — Happy pet + owner
- **Alt:** `static-pet-in-crate-safe.png` — Pet in crate (safe, calm) + “We fly them” subtext
- **Alt:** Phone with Telegram open + pet in frame

### CTA Button
- **Primary:** “Get Started” or “Open Telegram”
- **Link:** `https://t.me/FlyMyPetBot`

### Specs
- 1080×1080 (1:1) or 1080×1350 (4:5)
- Minimal text on image (Meta may throttle)
- High contrast for thumbnail clarity
