## Ad 1: Crisis / Abandonment (Legacy)
**File:** `ad-1-crisis.png`

### Psychological angle
- **Empathy + urgency**: Highlights the scale of the problem (hundreds of pets, homes waiting, no way to fly)
- **Injustice**: Adoptive homes exist but funds don’t — creates desire to fix an unfair situation
- **Sponsorship CTA**: “Sponsor a flight buddy” — low-friction way to help

### Target audience
- Potential sponsors in UAE and Europe
- People moved by animal welfare
- Rescuers and animal advocates

### Placement
- Rescue-focused posts, animal welfare communities
- UAE expat groups, pet forums

---

## Ad 2: Expat Guilt / Fear
**File:** `ad-2-expat.png`

### Psychological angle
- **Fear of abandonment**: “Don’t leave them behind” — speaks directly to relocation guilt
- **Loyalty**: Pet trusted you; framing leaving as betrayal
- **Relief**: Offers a path out (“We’ll get your pet to Europe”)

### Target audience
- UAE expats planning a move
- People who assumed they couldn’t bring their pet
- Anyone close to a relocation deadline

### Placement
- UAE expat Facebook/WhatsApp groups
- Moving/relocation communities
- LinkedIn (professionals in GCC)

---

## Ad 3: Hope / Reunion
**File:** `ad-3-reunited.png`

### Psychological angle
- **Hope + relief**: Shows the positive end state
- **Social proof**: “We make it possible” — trust in the outcome
- **Aspiration**: Reunion in Madrid — clear, desirable future

### Target audience
- People still hesitating about relocation
- Families in the middle of planning
- Anyone needing reassurance

### Placement
- Retargeting after seeing crisis/expat ads
- Testimonial and success-story content
- Mid-funnel, warm audiences

---

## Ad 4: Simplicity / Trust
**File:** `ad-4-simple.png`

### Psychological angle
- **Anxiety reduction**: “Just Telegram” — one simple action
- **Competence**: “We handle the rest” — removes burden of paperwork
- **Clarity**: Document review, export permits, flights listed — reduces perceived complexity

### Target audience
- People who were put off by bureaucracy
- Busy professionals
- Anyone who finds paperwork stressful

### Placement
- Cold traffic, broad audiences
- Ad-intro sequence
- Traffic to Telegram/landing page

---

## Crisis Context (GCC Pet Crisis)

- **Problem:** Many pets abandoned when expats leave UAE; rescues struggle with relocation costs
- **Opportunity:** Adoptive homes in Europe, but flights and paperwork block reunions
- **FlyMy.Pet role:** Document handling, permit coordination, flight booking, and rescue sponsorship

---

## Recommended Ad Sequencing

1. **Cold:** Ad 4 (Simplicity) or Ad 1 (Crisis) — attention + clarity
2. **Warm:** Ad 2 (Expat) — emotional connection
3. **Conversion:** Ad 3 (Reunion) — proof and hope
4. **Retarget:** Ad 4 (Simplicity) — remove last doubts
